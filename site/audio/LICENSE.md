# Recordings

The recordings here come from **audio-cmn** (https://github.com/hugolpz/audio-cmn), at its
24 kbit/s quality, renamed (`s/ban4.mp3` was `24k-abr/syllabs/cmn-ban4.mp3`; `w/中国.mp3`
was `24k-abr/hsk/cmn-中国.mp3`). They are licensed under the Creative Commons
Attribution-ShareAlike licence (CC BY-SA, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/),
like the originals:

* `s/` — every Mandarin syllable in every tone, read by **Chen Wang**, CC BY-SA.
* `w/` — the words and characters of the HSK (2000) lists, read by **Yue Tan**, from the
  Shtooka project's cmn-caen-tan collection (http://packs.shtooka.net/cmn-caen-tan/readme.txt),
  CC BY-SA.

Curated and compressed by Hugo Lopez (PLIDAM, INALCO); recording software by Nicolas Vion.
The rest of HanziHome is not under this licence; only these files are.
